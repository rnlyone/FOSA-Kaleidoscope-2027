Many pieces, one pattern

Like a kaleidoscope that turns many colourful pieces into one beautiful pattern, the FOSA 2027 Culture Show brings traditions from around the world onto one stage.

Read more

Every culture is unique. Together, they create something inspiring and beautiful.

“Tradition in Motion” reminds us that culture is not something frozen in the past. It is alive and evolving, and it keeps connecting people across generations and borders through music, dance, food, language and shared experiences.
